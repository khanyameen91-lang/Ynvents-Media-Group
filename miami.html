The Market

Where Miami events actually happen.

Miami isn't one meeting market — it's several, separated by causeways and traffic. Each one has its own venue profile and its own load-in reality.

Miami Beach & the Convention Center

The Miami Beach Convention Center anchors the market's largest trade shows and general sessions, surrounded by oceanfront resort ballrooms. Big productions, causeway logistics, and dock windows that don't move.

Brickell & Downtown

The financial district's hotels and corporate floors host investor days, board meetings, and launch events. Rooms are sharper and smaller, expectations are high, and the AV is meant to be invisible.

Coral Gables, Doral & Beyond

Association meetings, university and medical conferences, and social events run through the Gables and out toward Doral — often multi-day, multi-room, and heavier on breakout support than headline production.

Representative Build

What a typical hybrid conference looks like.

Scoping is always specific to your event, and yours will differ. But Miami draws a lot of conferences with an audience in the room and a larger one watching remotely — so here's the shape of a two-hundred-seat hybrid day, start to finish.

Scope Your Event Virtual & Hybrid
  • Pre-showVenue bandwidth tested and a hard-line connection confirmed. Hotel guest wifi is not a broadcast path, and finding that out on show day is too late.
  • Load-inAudio, lighting, and video built for the room first — the stream is fed from a proper program mix, not a camera at the back.
  • CameraMulti-camera positions set for stage, lectern, and audience Q&A, switched live so remote viewers see what matters.
  • CommsProducer on headset with your team and the venue. One person owns the timeline and calls the show.
  • RehearsalEvery presenter runs their deck on the actual switcher, with remote panelists dialed in and levels set before doors.
  • LiveIn-room mix and stream run in parallel with dedicated operators. Remote Q&A routed into the room so both audiences are in one conversation.
  • AfterRecording delivered per scope — full-session, per-speaker cuts, or clean feed for your own editing.

Miami FAQ

Questions we get from Miami planners.

How quickly can you turn around a Miami event?

Because our crews and inventory are local rather than trucked in, quoting and staffing don't wait on out-of-town equipment. Timelines still depend on scope and the venue's load-in window, but sourcing is rarely the constraint.

Do you cover Miami Beach and Coral Gables, or just downtown?

The whole market — Brickell, downtown, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Doral and the surrounding areas. We also travel nationwide for the right event.

Can you produce a hybrid conference with remote attendees?

Yes. Virtual and hybrid is a core service line. The in-room mix and the stream are designed together, so remote attendees get a broadcast feed rather than a camera pointed at a screen. More on virtual & hybrid.

What kinds of events do you handle in Miami?

Corporate general sessions and breakouts, hotel and ballroom events, trade shows, product launches, galas, weddings, and social events — from a 12-person boardroom to a 3,000-seat general session.

We manage a Miami hotel. What does an AV partnership involve?

A white-label, revenue-share arrangement: our technicians work under your brand, we carry the equipment investment, and your sales team sells the venue while ours sells the AV. See how partnerships work.
