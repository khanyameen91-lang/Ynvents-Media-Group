The YnventsIQ platform
The Real Differentiator

It understands what the hotel keeps and what you net.

Most event software assumes you're either the venue or the planner. YnventsIQ is built for the business in between: the AV company embedded inside someone else's property.

Venue Commission, Modeled Properly

Different commission rates per category, per property. Revenue is booked, the venue's cut is deducted, and a frozen periodic statement shows exactly what's owed — before a later edit can retroactively change it.

BEOs the Way Hotels Actually Issue Them

One numbered, signable Banquet Event Order per calendar day — even inside a multi-day event — generated straight from the function schedule, with built-in protection against double-billing a charge copied across days.
