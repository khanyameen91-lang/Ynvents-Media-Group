The Market

Where Fort Lauderdale events actually happen.

Broward's meeting business isn't concentrated in one building — it runs across three distinct zones, and each one asks something different of an AV team.

The Convention District

The Broward County Convention Center sits on the Intracoastal beside Port Everglades, and its expansion brought larger general sessions and trade-show floors into the market. Big rooms, long load-ins, tight dock schedules.

The Beach Corridor

The A1A hotel strip runs oceanfront meeting space and ballrooms built for incentive programs, board retreats, and galas. Smaller rooms, higher finish expectations, and far less tolerance for visible cable.

Downtown & Las Olas

Corporate offices, private clubs, and boutique properties around Las Olas host briefings, presentations, and social events at a scale where one over-specified line array does more harm than good.

Representative Build

What a typical 300-person general session looks like.

Every event is scoped on its own, and yours will differ. But if you've never commissioned AV before and want to know what you're actually buying, this is the shape of a standard hotel ballroom general session in Fort Lauderdale — from dock to strike.

Scope Your Event
  • Day beforeDock check and load-in. Staging and rigging points confirmed against the venue's plot before a single case is opened.
  • 6:00 AMLine array flown and time-aligned to the room. Ballrooms with hard surfaces get tuned, not preset.
  • 8:00 AMLighting focus — stage wash, uplighting to match brand color, gobo work if the deck calls for it.
  • 10:00 AMVideo: LED wall or projection built and mapped, presenter laptops tested on the actual switcher, not a lookalike.
  • 1:00 PMSpeaker rehearsals. Mic checks per presenter, slide advance confirmed, comms handed to the venue's event lead.
  • ShowA1 on audio, L1 on lighting, V1 on video, and a producer on comms with your team. No one is doing two of those jobs at once.
  • AfterStrike and out, on the venue's timeline. Recorded or streamed content delivered per whatever was scoped.
