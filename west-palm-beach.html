The Market

Where West Palm Beach events actually happen.

Palm Beach County runs a distinctive event calendar — heavier on philanthropy and seasonal programming than most South Florida markets, and spread across a long north-south corridor.

Downtown & the Convention Center

The Palm Beach County Convention Center and the surrounding downtown hotels carry conferences, trade shows, and the county's larger banquet programming, with Clematis Street and Rosemary Square feeding the after-hours side of multi-day events.

The Island & the Gala Season

Palm Beach's charity season concentrates a remarkable number of black-tie fundraisers into a few months. These rooms are unforgiving on aesthetics — the production has to disappear into the décor.

North County & Wellington

Palm Beach Gardens and Jupiter carry corporate and medical meetings, while Wellington's equestrian calendar brings its own run of hospitality and awards events on a seasonal cycle.

Representative Build

What a typical 250-guest gala looks like.

Every event is scoped on its own terms, and yours will differ. But fundraisers and awards evenings follow a recognizable shape, and if you're planning your first one, this is roughly what the AV side of the night involves.

Scope Your Event
  • Site visitRoom walked with the planner and venue. Sightlines, power, and rigging confirmed against the seating plan before design starts.
  • Load-inEarly build so the room can be flipped and dressed. Cable runs planned to stay out of guest sightlines and off the photography.
  • LightingUplighting to the event's color palette, stage wash for the podium and awards moments, and pin-spotting on centerpieces where the design calls for it.
  • AudioCoverage tuned for a room full of soft goods and conversation — speech intelligibility first, band or DJ playback second, and a spare handheld already live.
  • VideoScreens or LED for the mission video, sponsor recognition, and live auction totals — tested end to end, not just powered on.
  • ShowOperators on audio, lighting, and video, with a producer holding the run of show alongside your emcee and auctioneer.
  • StrikeOut on the venue's timeline, quietly, while the room is still clearing.
