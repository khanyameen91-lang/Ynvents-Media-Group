The Market

Where Boston events actually happen.

New England's meeting market is dense, historic, and logistically unforgiving — narrow streets, tight docks, and buildings that predate the idea of a freight elevator.

The Seaport & BCEC

The Boston Convention & Exhibition Center anchors the city's largest national meetings, surrounded by newer Seaport hotels with modern ballrooms and, mercifully, modern loading docks.

Back Bay & the Hynes

The Hynes Convention Center and the Back Bay hotel cluster carry mid-size conferences and association meetings in older, more characterful rooms — where rigging and power take genuine planning.

Cambridge & Kendall Square

The biotech, university, and technology corridor across the river runs symposia, investor days, and product launches — technical audiences with low tolerance for a presentation that doesn't cue cleanly.

Boston FAQ

Do you have a team and equipment based in Boston?

Yes to both. We have a full-time crew living and working in New England and house inventory stocked in the region — not a team and a truck dispatched from Florida for the week.

So who actually shows up to run my event?

Our own team, with our own equipment package scoped to your event. We work alongside established industry partners in the market where a build calls for additional resource, but the event is ours to deliver — we don't quietly hand it to an unknown subcontractor.

What size events do you handle in Boston?

From a 12-person boardroom briefing to a 3,000-seat general session, across corporate, hotel, academic, and social events.

Can you produce a hybrid or streamed event in Boston?

Yes. Virtual and hybrid production is a core service line, with the in-room mix and the stream built as one system rather than bolted together on the day. More on virtual & hybrid.
